Georgia Christakis, MD is a board certified pediatrician. After obtaining her medical degree at FSU in 2015, she completed her residency in pediatrics at Maria Fareri Children’s Hospital in Westchester, New York where she served an additional year as chief resident. She continued her education in Boston, completing a Global Pediatric Fellowship at Boston Children’s Hospital and additionally received her Master’s Degree in Public Health from Harvard University.
