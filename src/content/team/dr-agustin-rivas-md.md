As a board-certified pediatrician, civil surgeon, and scientific diver, Dr. Rivas has
dedicated his career to serving underserved communities, from North-west Alabama to Miami.
His extensive experience in pediatric and general medicine, coupled with his bilingual
proficiency in English and Spanish, has enabled him to deliver comprehensive care to pediatric
adolescent, and adult patients alike. Additionally, his commitment to environmental health is
evident through my work in restoring coral reefs in the Florida Keys as a scientific diver.

Dr. Rivas's passion for medicine transcends the clinical environment. He is a fervent advocate for
medical education and clinical research, constantly seeking innovative ways to enhance
healthcare delivery. The integration of telemedicine into his practice highlights his dedication to
making healthcare services more accessible. This commitment is further demonstrated by his
recent acceptance into the prestigious Climate and Health Equity Fellowship, which
underscores his focus on the nexus of environmental health and medicine. Moreover, Dr. Rivas
actively participates in the National Hispanic Medical Association Leadership Fellowship
Program, which aims to amplify his impact within the Hispanic community.

A significant aspect of Dr. Rivas's professional interests lies in the intersection of climate change
health, and equity. He is deeply invested in understanding and addressing the health impacts of
climate change, particularly how they affect vulnerable populations. This interest drives his
involvement in initiatives that seek to mitigate these impacts and promote health equity.
In addition to his clinical and environmental endeavors, he is actively involved with Doctors
Without Borders and the Dade County Street Response team. Through these organizations, he
provides free medical services to underserved and homeless populations, reinforcing his commitment to ensuring equitable healthcare access for all.

Since November 2014, he has served as the Medical Director at Sol Medical Center and
Sol Pediatrics in Miami, FL. In this role, Dr. Rivas oversees the operations of a general practice, ensuring
exemplary patient care and strict adherence to healthcare regulations. His leadership in this
capacity reflects an unwavering commitment to providing high-quality, accessible healthcare
to all.
