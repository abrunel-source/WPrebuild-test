Armen Henderson is a general internal medicine faculty member at the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine currently in a hybrid role of a hospitalist, educator, and community organizer. In 2022 he became a Climate Health and Equity Fellow the Medical Society Consortium.

His community facing responsibilities are Director of Health Programs with The Dream Defenders and Founder of the Dade County Street Response, which is the first free clinic in Miami's Liberty City. Additionally, his focus areas include climate change, disaster preparedness, street medicine, gun violence, and the social determinants of health.
