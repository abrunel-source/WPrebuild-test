As the impact of climate change is more clearly felt, we’re also starting to better understand the impacts it has on health. Working with communities to better protect against the harm of climate will likely be a key role for public health in the future. Dr Cheryl Holder of Florida Clinicians for Climate Action joined us at the Annual Meeting of the American Public Health Association to discuss more.
