mission: "The project trains doulas to assess climate change-related risks, particularly heat, for pregnant and postpartum women in high-heat vulnerability communities in Miami-Dade and Broward Counties. Doulas will learn to use tools like the CDC’s CHILL’D Out Screening and Heat and Health Tracker, create perinatal disaster action plans, and connect clients with resources such as portable air conditioners. They will also be equipped to educate community members about climate change impacts through monthly presentations at local health centers. This initiative aims to improve disaster preparedness and support vulnerable birthing people in coping with climate-related health risks."
